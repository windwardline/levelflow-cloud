The setup, level by level

Every setup is four prices. Entry is where the order waits. Stop is where the idea is wrong and the trade closes at a controlled loss. Target 1 and Target 2 are where profit gets taken — in two steps, not one.

The two-target plan

Set your take-profit at Target 2. When price reaches Target 1, close half and move your stop to your entry — profit locked either way.

Half the position banks real profit at the nearer target; the other half keeps working toward the farther one with the risk already removed. Your platform won't place this automatically — the Desk keeps every level on screen so you can make the two moves by hand when Target 1 hits.

What the words mean here

Bank half
Close half your position and take that profit now.
Move your stop to your entry
Change the stop-loss order to the price you entered at — from here the worst case is breaking even on what's left.
Pending
Your order is placed but hasn't filled yet. Nothing to do.
