# R4 act 2 — design (v3, 2026-09-02; v2 reviewed by the statistical lens, dispositions in §Review)

## Mandate and bindings (from the record)
- R4: every matched market individually, against its OWN shipped configuration, absolute expectancy as the criterion (HANDOFF R4 row; remediation Phase 4).
- D4's rule binds per market: accepted = beatsBaseline && earnsMoney; earnsMoney = the variant's own SELECT-fold expectancy positive beyond its 95% lower bound.
- Amendment 39: profit is the measure; a rate sits beside money; yardstick ladderRewardRisk.
- Amendment 36: withdrawal only on genuinely data-derived evidence that survives removal of our own modelling choices (the GROSS arm exists for this); withdrawal is never permanent.
- M3: the confirm read reports confirmed-profitable / contradicted / indistinguishable with a 95% interval and the delta beside it; never decides.
- LA-6: one ledgered read; selecting on confirm is fitting to the held-back data; the read is the owner's to authorize.

## The five deliverables of act 2

### 1. Per-market grading on the tuning folds (the instrument, then the run)
- Facts (lens 2): `--per-market-folds` re-cuts each market's span from the MANIFEST's series facts at 50/75% and relabels rows by time, discarding `row.split`. Under the seal it never creates a market-local confirm cell (no market's local 75% point precedes its emitted confirm start on either R3 corpus) — its only live effects are demoting emitted SELECT rows into local FIT (median 152 days on global folds; 16 markets lose their whole select fold) and dropping containment-crossers. Under `--confirm-final` it INVERTS: a median 1,278 days (global) / 329 days (classfolds) of the HELD-BACK fold is relabelled into local select and drives acceptance. A market-local confirm cell, had one appeared, would be dropped ungraded, unprinted, unledgered and uncounted. The re-cut cannot deliver a clean market-local third.
- Design: RETIRE the time re-cut for grading. Fold source is the EMITTED per-class label, always. `--per-market-folds` is refused by name with the reason and the replacement; `--verdict-unit market` (already a gradeCorpus option, `verdictUnit`) is the per-market grain. derive-4d and confirm-4d follow (they pass `perMarketFolds` today).
- Corpus: `docs/research/r3/capture-all-classfolds.jsonl` (every class has tuning rows; the global-fold corpus's 2023-era markets have no tuning rows at all).
- Grain: one market at a time (singleton groups); every grid variant vs the market's shipped cell (`baseline`); the gate's existing statistics — paired day-block permutation (descriptive), family-wise max-T sign-flip, MIN_EFFECTIVE_PAIRS 5, thin rule, D4's earnsMoney = select-fold 95% lower bound > 0; 30-filled floor. Class-grain pooling refused on a per-class spec.
- The SHIPPED cell itself is graded absolutely: per market, select-fold expectancy net AND gross with 95% CI and n; `shippedEarnsSelect` (lower bound > 0) beside it. A shipped cell that fails is FLAGGED `shipped-not-earning` — a decline candidate under amendment 36 (gross beside net), never a decision.
- Holdout: no market is dropped; the stratified held-out set is LABELLED (deliverable 4).
- Output: `docs/research/r4/per-market-grading-<arm>.json` via `writeResearchArtifact`, plus a stdout table; never a confirm figure.

### 2. The one ledgered read, extended (code + fixtures; NOT run)
- Facts (lens 2): confirm-4d's artifact today carries per-pick `confirmTotalDelta, confirmFilled, confirmBaseFilled, confirmExpectancy(+Lower/Upper), confirmExpectancyDelta(+Lower), gateDisposition, gateReason, variant`, with no corpus hash, no identity, and figures from `realizedR` only — there is no gross confirm figure anywhere. threshold-rescue needs a CURVE over thresholds 0..95, which is a selection on the held-back fold if served from confirm.
- Design: under `--confirm-final`, gradeCorpus additionally reads, per market, the SHIPPED cell's absolute confirm expectancy NET and GROSS (from `realizedR` / `grossRealizedR`) with 95% CI and n filled, for EVERY market in the corpus (held-out ones labelled), inside the same ledgered read; accepted variants keep their deltas with CI and M3 outcomes. The artifact carries `corpusHashes`, `identity` (conditionsOf), `readId`, `readAt`, the ledger line's key, `verdictUnit`, `foldSource: "emitted"`, the holdout rule and the named set, and the figures.
- Consumers: roster-expectancy-audit takes each market's absolute confirm figure (net) from the artifact and refuses an INVALID banner or a corpus-hash mismatch against the corpus it read for select; cost-sensitivity-verdict takes the GROSS confirm figure for amendment 36's precondition with the same refusals; threshold-rescue consumes NOTHING from confirm — it is a select-only screening instrument whose proposed thresholds become a supplementary grid arm (confidenceThreshold is a grid axis) and are then confirmed, if at all, as accepted variants through the one read. Until an artifact exists for a corpus, all three judge on select and say so (as today).

- Recommendation: ONE exported helper `heldOutMarkets(manifests)` = the stratified rule over the shard union, called by every reader that excludes anything and by the gate; the stamped row field and `holdoutSymbols` demoted to provenance (readers may print both, never exclude on the stamp). ## Pre-registered rules (frozen before any read; hashed into the artifact)

- **Decline candidate (amendment 36's cost clause):** a market is a decline candidate when, on the select fold of the per-class corpus, its shipped cell's NET expectancy 95% upper bound < 0 AND its GROSS expectancy 95% upper bound < 0, with ≥ 30 filled in select. Anything else is not a candidate. The confirm read REPORTS, per M3, whether the shipped cell's confirm net expectancy is confirmed-negative (upper bound < 0), contradicted (lower bound > 0) or indistinguishable — for candidates AND non-candidates alike, so the rule cannot be re-fitted to the read.
- **Accept candidate:** the gate's existing rule (beatsBaseline && earnsMoney) per market on the emitted folds; the confirm read reports M3's three outcomes for accepted variants only, as today.
- **No second decision:** nothing chooses a threshold, a variant or a decline after the read; the read's artifact is consumed by readers that print, never by readers that pick.
