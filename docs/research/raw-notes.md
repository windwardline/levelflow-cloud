Title: E8 Signature Futures | E8 Markets Help Center Futures

"This is our 1-Step challenge - designed exclusively for Futures traders who value speed, clarity, and control"

SimFi Challenge rules (Phase 1 / evaluation):
- Profit Target - Closed profit in your simulated trading account.
  - $1,500 Profit Target - $25,000 account
  - $3,000 Profit Target - $50,000 account
  - $6,000 Profit Target - $100,000 account
  - $9,000 Profit Target - $150,000 account
- Inactivity rule - "no time limit, however, after 7 days of inactivity, the account is disabled."
- EOD Dynamic drawdown – "A moving loss limit based on your highest end-of-day balance. It only updates once per day at market close (intraday equity swings do not move it). It locks permanently at the initial balance level."
  - $1,000 EOD - $25,000 account
  - $2,000 EOD - $50,000 account
  - $3,000 EOD - $100,000 account
  - $4,500 EOD - $150,000 account

SimFI Performance rules (funded stage):
- 80% profit split ("eligible to receive 80% of their trading performance")
- Inactivity rule same (7 days)
- Daily pause – "A soft daily loss limit based on your starting balance of the day. If your floating or closed loss reaches 2%, trading stops until the next day. Your account is not breached. It simply pauses and resets at midnight."
  - $500 Daily Pause - $25,000 account
  - $1,000 Daily Pause - $50,000 account
  - $2,000 Daily Pause - $100,000 account
  - $3,000 Daily Pause - $150,000 account
- EOD Dynamic drawdown - same def as above, same $ amounts as above ($1,000/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500)

Payout criteria:
- 35% Best Day rule - "you must ensure that no single trading day exceeds more than 35% of your total generated profits."
- Minimum payout $100. "with 80% payout, you need to request at least $125" (net profit basis)
- Minimum profitable days between each payout: 5 (does not apply to first payout)
- "A profitable day is considered as a day where the realized closed PnL equals to 0.3% or more."
  - $75 - $25,000 account (=0.3%)
  - $150 - $50,000 account
  - $300 - $100,000 account
  - $450 - $150,000 account
- Payout buffer - "Before you can start requesting payout, you need to leave a buffer in your account in the same size as your EOD Dynamic Drawdown. The buffer can not be requested." Example: "If your EOD is 3% and your initial balance $100,000, the buffer is $3000."
- Maximum amount of payouts per account: 5. After 5 payouts "the cycle is closed, the account is deactivated, and you move on with the free Challenge of the same size." — "This rule applies only to the Signature Futures account purchased after 14.07.2026 20:00 UTC +2"
- Payout Mechanism: "only profit earned during the current cycle is eligible. ... Unused profits from previous cycles don't carry over." — same 14.07.2026 20:00 UTC+2 cutoff applies.
- Payout caps table (Account size columns: $25,000 | $50,000 | $100,000 | $150,000):
  - 1. Payout: $1,000 | $1,250 | $2,250 | $3,250
  - 2. Payout: $1,000 | $1,250 | $2,250 | $3,250
  - 3. Payout: $1,250 | $2,250 | $3,250 | $4,250
  - 4. Payout: $1,250 | $2,250 | $3,250 | $4,250
  - 5+ payout: $1,500 | $3,250 | $4,250 | $5,250

Trading hours:

- "It is not possible to hold positions overnight on the E8 Zero Futures model." [NOTE: sentence appears inside Signature Futures article - possibly copy artifact referencing Zero model; flag as-is]
