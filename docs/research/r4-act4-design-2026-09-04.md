# R4 act 4 — the populations act 3 could not decide (design, 2026-09-04)

Act 3's one ledgered read left three populations undecided and named each one.
This act decides the two that can be decided at the protected anchor, and
scopes the third. It changes no calibration; every figure it produces is
graded on the tuning folds and the confirm fold stays sealed until a read the
program pre-registers.

## What act 3 left, with the money

| population | n | what the read said | money on the fold |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| **unnominated** | 11 | confirmed negative on the confirmation fold, but the select fold never nominated them, so declining them would have been dredged from the fold | **−4,125R** over 23,575 fills |
| **late-listed** | 22 | every row falls inside their class's confirm fold, so no tuning-fold verdict exists at all | not measurable yet |
| **contradicted cells** | 46 of 91 | the shipped cell loses on the held-back fold — crypto 32 of 33, the four forex majors | the desk cannot unpark on this layer |

The eleven are BTCUSD, ETHUSD, USDJPY, XRPUSD, LINKUSD, SP, ZFUSD, ARWUSD,
BNBUSD, THETAUSD and TRUMPUSD.

## Why a re-sweep is required, and what it costs

The engine changed twice on 2026-09-03. One change is corpus-visible and one
is not, and the difference decides the design:

- **The forex cost cap IS visible.** `maxCostShare: 0.15` declines forex setups
  whose modelled round trip exceeds 15% of the risk unit — measured at 4.78% of
  forex admissions on the tuning folds. Every existing corpus was swept without
  it, so every forex figure in them describes a configuration production no
  longer runs.
- **The register is NOT visible.** `sweep.ts` never consults
  `ENGINE_DECLINED_MARKETS`; `replay-sweep.ts` records it in the manifest as
  provenance only. A declined market still emits rows, which is what makes the
  register re-decidable from a corpus at all.

So one capture-all sweep at the protected anchor under
`2026.09.03.register-redecision` re-bases every figure on what production runs.
**Cost: zero provider bytes** — the anchored preflight at depth 7000 over the
97-market roster reports 313 cache artifacts all carrying the 2026-08-26 pin,
97 markets warm, 0.00 GiB spent, so the run cannot reach the provider.

## Act 4a — the baseline re-base and the two decidable populations

One `--capture-all` sweep, anchor 2026-08-26, depth 7000, the full roster, with
an **emit-time per-market fold spec** for the 22 late-listed markets. Emit-time
because a read-time re-cut is what the 2026-08-11 defect did: it demoted emitted
select rows into a local fit and relabelled a median 329 days of the held-back
fold into select, where acceptance is decided. The fold edges belong to the
emitter.

From that one corpus:

1. **Nominate the eleven properly.** Grade each on its own rows at the market
   unit and apply `DECLINE_RULE` on the SELECT fold — net and gross upper bounds
   below zero at 30 filled. A market that nominates there is eligible for the
   register's ENTER clause at the next read; one that does not is cleared and
   stays live, and the record says which and why.
2. **Give the 22 a tuning-fold verdict at all.** With their own fold edges they
   have fit and select rows for the first time, so their shipped cells can be
   graded and their decline candidacy decided on the same rule as everyone
   else's.
3. **Re-grade all 91 shipped cells on the new engine.** The forex figures move;
   nothing else should. Any non-forex cell whose figure moves is a defect in
   this act's instrument, not a finding, and the act refuses rather than
   reporting it.

## Act 4b — the contradicted cells (scoped here, run after 4a)

46 shipped cells lose on the held-back fold. Re-deriving them is a per-market
grid over the axes act 3 left open, and its size depends on what 4a's re-base
shows: cells whose figures move under the cost cap are re-derived first, and the
crypto layer — 32 of 33 markets contradicted — is the largest single question.
4a's evidence sets 4b's grid, so 4b is designed after 4a lands and not before.

## The read, and what it costs to open the fold twice

Act 4's confirm fold overlaps act 3's recorded calendar on every shared symbol,
so any read runs under `--acknowledge-prior-reads` with the leaked bit named:
for the 46 cells act 3 already confirmed negative, a second read of the same
dates is not independent evidence, and the act may not present it as if it
were. What IS independent: the eleven's select-fold nomination (act 3 never
nominated them), the 22's tuning-fold figures (act 3 could not compute them),
and every figure on rows the cost cap newly declines.

**No read runs in 4a.** The nominations and the tuning-fold verdicts are select
work. A read is pre-registered only when a candidate exists that a read could
confirm.

## Pre-registered refusals

- The instrument refuses if any non-forex shipped cell's select figure moves
  beyond floating-point tolerance between the R3 corpus and this one: the only
  intended difference is the forex cap.
- The fold spec is refused unless every late-listed market's fit and select
  folds each hold at least 30 filled outcomes; below that the market is
  reported as starved, which is a verdict about the configuration (amendment
  25), not about the market.
- The sweep is refused if the anchored preflight reports any artifact without
  the 2026-08-26 pin.
