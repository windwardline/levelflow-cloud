# The feed window, round 2 — what falls, what stands, and the remedy (2026-09-07)

Round 1 priced the banked share and found the forex window underneath it.
This round put the window itself to three dimensions of refuters — what it
does to act 3's verdicts, what the law says about it, and what it does to the
program — two independent refuters each. The verify phase died on the account
limit, so **every deciding figure below was reproduced by the driver's own
pass before it was written here**, with the frozen candidate figures as its
control (`docs/research/r3/cap-era-split-2026-09-07.txt`; the script is
`cap-era-split.py` in the toolchain directory). Where a claim is the
refuters' and not yet re-measured, it says so.

## 1. What falls

**"Forex the only net-positive class" cannot stand.** The claim is the R3
re-sweep's (`r3-one-resweep-2026-09-02.md`) and rides in HANDOFF's R3 row as
`+0.006R ±0.005 per filled trade (+2,896.9R over fit+select)`. On the
witness's contained years the same population reads:

| fold | fills | net R |
|---|---:|---:|
| fit (entirely contained) | 174,503 | −94.7 |
| select, contained | 55,419 | −1,321.1 |
| **fit + select, contained** | **229,922** | **−1,415.8 (−0.0062 R/fill)** |
| select, escaping | 23,513 | +2,925.0 (+0.1244 R/fill) |

A 4,312.7 R swing, and a sign change: every dollar forex makes at the shipped
ladder sits in the years the witness names. Arithmetic on the driver's own
tracked table (`contained-years-capture-all-classfolds.txt`, #590). Under
amendments 34 and 39 the desk may not be told forex is net-positive at any
grain.

**The S8 stop cell's verdict cannot stand.** `maxStopAtrMultiplier=8` with
the daily source — "the only swept stop cell accepted anywhere" (§6b of the
act-3 note; frozen fit +2.2 R, select +247.8 R) — reads −196.4 R on 2018–2020
select and +444.2 R on 2021–2022H1: 179 % of the accepted delta is window
money, and every clean year is negative. Both refuters measured it; the
driver has not re-measured it, and nothing shipped from it (forex
`maxStopAtrMultiplier` stays 1.0), so the correction is to the record only.

## 2. What stands — the cost cap, measured

Act 3's one confirmed verdict is `costShareMax=0.15` on forex. The driver's
pass reproduces the frozen figures exactly (fit +563.6595, select +306.1525)
and then splits them:

| fold | bucket | base fills | base R | declined | ΔR | kept E | kept lo95 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fit | contained (all of it) | 174,503 | −94.7 | 7,466 | **+563.7** | +0.0028 | −0.0002 |
| select | all | 78,932 | +1,603.8 | 5,662 | +306.2 | +0.0261 | +0.0216 |
| select | contained | 55,419 | −1,321.1 | 5,194 | **+353.7** | **−0.0193** | **−0.0248** |
| select | escaping | 23,513 | +2,925.0 | 468 | −47.5 | +0.1249 | +0.1179 |

**The delta is a clean-years property and the window diluted it** — +353.7 R
on contained select against +306.2 all-years, positive on 17 of 22 pool
markets there and ≤ 0 on 20 of 22 escaping. The mechanism explains it: the
window's inflated intraday range widens `riskDistance` and lowers the cost
share, so the cap barely fires in those years (468 of 23,513, against 5,194
of 55,419).

**The cap keeps trading, and the reason is money, not amendment 36.** An
earlier draft of this note cited amendment 36 here. That was a category
error, caught in review: amendment 36 is the withdrawal standard for a
MARKET leaving the offering, and nothing here withdraws a market. Whether a
calibration parameter stays set is D4's jurisdiction and amendment 39's. So
price it. Unsetting the cap returns forex's contained select fold from
−967.4 R over 50,225 fills to **−1,321.1 R over 55,419** — it costs the desk
**353.7 R on clean rows and worsens expectancy by 0.0046 R/fill**, on exactly
the data the desk trades live (forex's escaping years end in 2023–2024; 2025
and 2026 are contained on every pair). Under amendment 39 the cap stays
because removing it loses money, not because a standard protects it.

**What cannot stand is the earns-money clause — and what it really says.**
The class-grain accept read the capped stream's own select expectancy at
+0.026 R/fill (95 % lower +0.022). On contained rows it is **−0.0193
[−0.0248, −0.0138]** over 50,225 fills — D4's own "LOSES MONEY" wording, the
clause act 3 refused crypto and futures under. Two things follow, and the
second is the important one.

*First*: the record's "the gate's own earns-money term passed at the class
grain" is true of a mixed-regime fold and false of clean years. Corrected at
every carrier.

*Second*: **the term does not discriminate here.** The UNCAPPED forex stream
reads −0.0238 R/fill on the same contained rows — worse. D4's earns-money
test fails for the cap and for its absence alike, so it cannot be read as a
verdict on the cap. What it is a verdict on is forex itself: on clean years
the class loses money capped or uncapped, and the cap is the smaller loss.
If the cap were proposed today as a NEW candidate on contained rows it would
not pass D4, and neither would anything else forex currently ships. That is
the finding, and it is a finding about the calibration, not about this
filter.

The confirm read itself was made **harder** to pass, not easier: its fold is
45.2 % escaping by date count (44,044 of 97,522 accepted baseline forex rows
in the verdict pool — a date count only, no outcome or money field read;
`docs/research/r3/cap-era-split-2026-09-07.txt`), and on escaping rows the
cap's tuning-fold delta is negative.

## 3. The remedy — decided

The three options that went into the round were R1 (readers refuse escaping
rows), R2 (stratify, and the ledgered read refuses or flags a cell whose
confirm rows are majority-escaping) and R3 (a fold calendar that avoids the
years). None survives as written, and the reasons are measurements:

- **All three are framed at the class grain, which amendment 33 forbids** —
  "per market, never per class". The map is already per symbol-year.
- **R2's trigger never fires and flips with the grain.** The forex confirm
  fold is 44,044 of 97,522 escaping = 45.2 % by year grain (under any
  majority) and 51 % by month grain. A rule whose verdict depends on the
  grain of its own denominator is not a rule.
- **R3 is unreachable and would change nothing.** The stores are a fresh
  2026-08-23/24 fetch, both intraday tiers carry the window and the daily
  does not, so a refetch returns the same bytes — and the 5-minute endpoint
  has returned HTTP 402 since 2026-09-04.
- **R1 uniformly would starve classes** whose escaping years are most of
  their history (crypto 2017–2020).

**The remedy, adopted:** for the **next** read, per-market span exclusion of
a market's own escaping years by default, opt-in to include with the label
printed — which is what amendment 33 already asks for ("spans are measured
first, per market, per timeframe, and recorded before anything consumes
them"), and the year map is exactly that span record. For **existing**
verdicts, stratification: every forex figure is reported on contained years
beside all years, which the readers now do (#590).

**What that costs to build, stated honestly.** The stratified half exists
today: `scripts/feedYears.ts` labels rows after a sweep and three readers
consume it. The exclusion half does not. `scripts/sweepFolds.ts` builds one
continuous span per class (`corpusStartMs`..`corpusEndMs`) and cuts fit,
select and confirm from it; forex's escaping years sit in the MIDDLE of that
span, so excluding them means excising a hole from a fold calendar, which
nothing in the sweep can do today. Round 2 adopts the rule; building it is
its own change set, and the note that claims the map "is exactly that span
record" means the map is the input such a build would read, not that the
build is done.

**One caveat holds the map back from gating anything today.** The witness's
per-UTC-year binary map is ragged at the reversion edge: the refuters measured
2024-01..07 running at 1.27–1.80× the baseline bar-range ratio on nine of the
twelve stores whose 2024 the witness leaves contained, with 2024-08..12 back
at 0.80–0.92×. The onset is one month and the reversion is not year-aligned.
The month-grain confirm share the refuters report (51 % against the year
grain's 45.2 %) is the same measurement and is likewise theirs, not
re-measured here.
Before the map gates a read it needs a finer grain than the year. The driver
has not re-measured this; it is the refuters'.

## 4. On amendment 36 — a correction to the driver's own writing

The witness note of 2026-09-06 said "amendment 36's standard runs both ways:
a market may not be withdrawn on a flawed input of our own making, and a
market may not be confirmed on one either." The second half is the driver's
extension, not the amendment's text. Amendment 36 names "a flawed parameter
of our own making — a window, a cap, a modeled spread, a sampled cost" and
governs **withdrawal** only. A provider's feed is not a parameter we chose.
What governs a confirmation is amendment 33 (per market, to each market's
true data limit) with amendment 39 (profit is the measure). The corrected
statement: the window does not withdraw anything and does not by itself void
a confirmation; it means a verdict priced on those bars is not a verdict
about the market the desk trades, and every such verdict is restated on
contained years.

## 5. Consequences for the record, and what does not change

The corpus stays valid: its bytes are what the provider served, the rebuild
reproduced the digest, and the readers stratify. No INVALID banner, no
rebuild, no re-sweep, no register entry (the register holds no forex market).
The act-3 artifact stays valid — `readLedgeredArtifact` refuses an INVALID
one, and marking it so would delete the provenance of a filter worth +917 R
on clean tuning rows.

The desk stays parked. What still blocks a new calendar is outside the
repository: the FMP intraday plan (HTTP 402 since 2026-09-04). A first-read
forex confirm fold needs bars after 2026-08-26, which cannot accrue at 402.
