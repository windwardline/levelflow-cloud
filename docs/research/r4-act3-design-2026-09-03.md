Three corrections to the act-2 record: seventeen candidates are crypto, not

ΔR > 0 and their fit ΔR is 0 by construction. The late-listed problem is 22
markets on this calendar, not six.

Where the money sits: class rows cap the stop at 1.0 ATR (metals 1.6) below
the 1.25-ATR floor, so every one of the 25 class-row markets runs clamped
(stop provenance 100% cap); the derived layer chose cap 4 on 65 markets and
2.5 on 6, and at cap 4 the cap still binds on 8–40% of fills. Class-row
candidates carry a median cost share of 0.32–0.66 of R: the cap IS their
cost lever. Cost share ranks realized R strongly on the fit fold (ρ +0.42

quartile is near break-even on several losers); the composite confidence

variant clears one clustered SE.

## 2. Pre-registration measurements (tuning folds only; confirm never read)

- **Confidence threshold — no money.** 46 of 91 shipped cells have a
  select-positive threshold, 28 at 0; of the 18 above zero, 14 keep 1–44% of
  fills (THIN) and the four that keep at least half move +36R combined.
  The score does not rank money (§1). Dropped as an axis.
- **Admission floor — the money.** Fills admitted at a net payoff
  (`rewardRisk`, the runner target's ratio after the modelled round trip;
  `sweep.ts:1028`) of 1.2–1.5 lose in every class on gross as well as net.
  On FIT alone: a floor of 1.5 adds crypto +440, forex +305, futures +245,
  agriculture +239, metals +237 (keeping 93/97/91/62/59% of fills); 1.6 adds
  more in crypto (+560), futures (+507), agriculture (+336), metals (+358)
  and less in forex (+285 at 69% kept); 1.7 loses in crypto and forex. On
  select, 1.5 improves 71 of 91 markets and refuses three as THIN (WTI 29%,
  ZMUSD 34%, ZSUSX 35% kept). Values {1.5, 1.6} were first seen with both
  folds in view; the FIT-only reading gives the same two, and that is
  stated rather than hidden. The floor gates the runner ratio, not
  `ladderRewardRisk`; no printed ratio is a term of any verdict.

## 4. Arm F: derived variants in the gate

**Built (results PR).** `grid-totalr --derive-filters "name:field>=value;…"`;
reproduce-first executed: a floor at the baseline's own value reproduces the
baseline cell exactly, and each variant's figures equal an independent sum
over the kept rows; a predicate inverted by mutation fails both. Graded at
the market unit (this design's grain) and, because a uniform admission knob
ships per class, at the class unit as well; both are in the record §6a.

`confidenceThreshold` and `minRewardRisk` are post-hoc filters on
`confidenceScore` and `plan.rewardRisk` with no inter-decision state
(`sweep.ts:1026-1032`); both fields are on every row; R3 proved the gated arm
≡ capture-all filtered on `accepted`. A sweep would reproduce rows the corpus
already holds. The gate gains `--derive-filter <name>=<field><op><value>`:
for each baseline row it adds a derived variant whose `accepted` is the
baseline's AND the predicate, then grades it under its own rule unchanged
(pairing by day, permutation, THIN, D4). Reproduce-first: a derived floor at
each class's shipped `minRewardRisk` must reproduce the baseline cell
exactly (identity), and the 1.5 / 1.6 variants must reproduce the scratch
counterfactual's per-class fit and select totals (an external anchor computed
independently). The artifact names each derived variant's predicate and
hashes it. Cells: `payoffFloor=1.5`, `payoffFloor=1.6`. The cost-share rule
(`estimatedRoundTripCost/riskDistance ≤ x`, x chosen on FIT) is graded the
same way as a third and fourth derived variant so the read can compare the
two admission instruments — it has no engine knob yet, and shipping it live
is engine work after the read.

## 5. Grading, retirement, freezing, the read

- Grade every arm at the market unit on the emitted per-class folds
  (`grid-totalr --verdict-unit market --out docs/research/r4/<arm>-grading.json`).
  The artifact now carries every variant's own select figures, net and
  gross, in the shipped cell's shape (code PR).
- **Retirement rule, pre-registered:** a decline candidate retires from
  candidacy iff some cell of S or W with ≥ 30 filled and ≥ 50% of the shipped
  cell's select fills fails DECLINE_RULE (net upper ≥ 0 OR gross upper ≥ 0).
  The retiring cell, its n and both figures are recorded; retirement by one
  cell of k is labelled fragile; a retired market ships unchanged and its M3
  is still reported. The multiplicity points toward retention, which is
  amendment 31's asymmetry, stated.
- **The 16 no-fit markets:** unmeasurable for acceptance on this calendar;
  retirement (select-based) is still tested; their shipped cells are read
  absolutely. Recommendation for the owner (§8): a per-market fold spec for
  the 22 late-listed markets as act 4, a corpus of 22 markets.
- **Freeze** (`scripts/freeze-candidates.ts`, code PR): per market, among
  the variants the gate accepted in any arm, the largest fit ΔR (ties: the
  smaller paired p, then arm, then variant); refuses a grading that opened
  the confirm fold, arms that disagree on anchor/engine/calendar/holdout, and
  — added — arms whose shipped-cell select figures are not bit-identical (a
  cross-arm choice needs one baseline). Records per market the cells tested
  across arms and the program's expected false accepts (0.05 × cells tested).
- **The read** consumes the frozen file. **Built (results PR):**
  `grid-totalr --confirm-final --verdict-unit market --frozen <frozen-candidates.json> <every arm's corpus>`
  opens THE one baseline — taken from the first corpus and verified
  row-for-row against every other corpus's baseline (count, R and gross R per
  market and fold, the confirm fold included and never printed) — and each
  market's frozen candidate from its own arm (a derived candidate is rebuilt
  from the baseline rows under the predicate the frozen file carries,
  hash-checked and parented on the baseline), prunes every other arm's
  candidate from a market's verdicts, relaxes only the grid term of the shard
  conditions, binds every corpus to the emit digest its arm's grading
  recorded, records each candidate's own disposition, and records the frozen
  hash on the one ledger line and in the read artifact. It refuses a run that
  is not confirm-final, a class-unit run, a shard no arm bound, an arm whose
  corpus is missing, a corpus whose bytes differ from the graded ones, a
  candidate that contributes no rows, a derived name meaning two predicates,
  derive-filters passed beside it (and a derived read of the fold that
  nothing froze), and a tampered or re-ruled frozen file. Identity is
  pinned: over one corpus it equals the plain confirm-final read figure for
  figure. The mutation that dropped the arm check is killed by a test that
  counts a candidate's fills from one arm only. The read stays one command,
  after this act, with a vetted package.
- **The retirement rule lives in the freeze** (`--removal-arms S,W`): per
  decline candidate it records the removal-arm cells tested (≥ 30 filled and
  ≥ 50% of the shipped cell's select fills), the retiring cells with both
  figures, and the fragile label; its rule text and hash are in the frozen
  file and the read refuses a file frozen under another. Mutations that
  dropped the gross clause or the sample floor are killed by its tests.
