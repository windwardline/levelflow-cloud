Which leg of stopBuffer binds — second pass, same population and the same doors.

stopBuffer = max(atr * stopAtrMultiplier, dailyAtr * dailyStopAtrMultiplier)
For forex that resolves to max(atr * 1.2, dailyAtr * 0.12), so the DAILY leg binds
exactly when dailyAtr / atr > 10. `atr` is a 14-bar intraday ATR on the 15-minute
series; `dailyAtr` is a 14-DAY ATR.
