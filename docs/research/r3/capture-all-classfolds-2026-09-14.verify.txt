WHAT THAT MEANS FOR THE READ
  The re-simulate is the corpus of record measured under the SHIPPED engine's
  admission, not a copy of the record. Its `accepted` carries the cost-share cap
  that the record's readers apply post hoc, and its four USD-quote pairs carry the
  commission the engine charges today. The eight-cell control was therefore
  re-derived on it (r3/hour-mechanism-2026-09-14.txt) rather than taken from
  2026-09-07, and every cell of the new table reconciles to the old one, to the
  row and to the printed tenth of an R:

    new n     = record n     − rows the cap removed              (exact, all eight cells)
    new net R = record net R − the R those rows carried + the four pairs' repricing
                                                                  (to within 0.1 R)

  Six of the eight close on the printed tenth; two are 0.1 R off because the
  three summands are themselves printed to a tenth. The identity closes at full
  precision.

  The eight-cell reconciliation is printed in full in
  r3/re-simulate-attribution-2026-09-14.txt and in §2 of the record of the read.
